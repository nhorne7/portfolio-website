How it works: 2-week sprints

The course is built from 2-week sprints. Each sprint is a single subject at a single level — Physics or Maths — taught as its own small cohort. Physics and Maths run in parallel during the same window, so you can take one or enrol in both. Five live sessions per week, ten per sprint, roughly 90 minutes each.

Questions, answered

What if my school uses a different syllabus order?

Sprint topics are confirmed with enrolled students beforehand, so we can align the focus with your school's sequence or prioritise the units you find hardest.

SL or HL — does it matter which I pick?

Cohorts are grouped by level so the pace fits. If you're unsure, choose "Not sure yet" on the form and we'll figure out the right fit together.

Can I take both Physics and Maths?

Yes. They run in separate time slots so you can enrol in both — and adding a second subject saves you 15% on that enrollment.

What if I miss a session?

Every live session is recorded and shared with the cohort, so you can catch up on anything you miss.

How are payments handled?

By SEPA bank transfer against a proper invoice. You enquire here first; nothing is charged until your slot is confirmed.
